================================================================================
API ENDPOINTS
================================================================================

The extension integrates with Video-AI's REST API:

1. Authentication:
   POST /api/v1/auth/verify
   Authorization: Bearer {token}

2. Start Processing:
   POST /api/v1/videos/process
   Body: {video_url, user_interests[], output_length}
   Response: {job_id}

3. Check Status:
   GET /api/v1/jobs/{job_id}
   Polling interval: 2 seconds
   Max retries: 180 (6 hours)
   Timeout: 30 seconds
